# Screenshots

> **Previews only.** Every image here is a mock-up with sample data, drawn to match the addon's real layout and look. None are in-game captures: WoW: Forever launches November 4, 2026. The Quests & Items, guides and Ask LOMB shots use the bundled ForeverDB.net snapshot through the offline test harness (Data from ForeverDB.net · https://foreverdb.net); the characters, guildmates, drops and progress in them are sample data.

All shots show v0.10.0 in the storybook look, the default. [Back to the README](../README.md)
